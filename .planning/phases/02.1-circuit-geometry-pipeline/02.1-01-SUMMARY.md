## Decisions & Deviations

### Decisions
- **Curvature preservation:** GPS-derived curvature was rejected (noise produces R=10m where reality is R=45m). Hand-authored curvature from published corner radii is re-indexed proportionally to the new distance array.
- **Source data caching:** External data files committed to repo (<100KB each) for offline reproducibility and provenance tracking.
- **Periodic spline fitting:** Used `splprep(s=0, per=True, k=3)` for exact interpolation through all source points with periodic boundary conditions, resampled to uniform ~5m spacing.
