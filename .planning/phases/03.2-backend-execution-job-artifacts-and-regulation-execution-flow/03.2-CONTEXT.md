<vision_impact>
## Vision Impact

**What long-horizon capability this phase protects or unlocks:**

- **Transport-neutral execution boundary** — preserves the LONG-ARC seam that lets compute later move between local-worker, sidecar, remote, and cloud backends without rewriting the product surface. Without this phase, the synchronous route-handler path hardens and becomes an implicit "this product only ever runs synchronously on one machine" commitment. (LONG-ARC § Protected Seams 1.)
- **Typed artifact identity with lineage, provenance, fidelity, validation, and comparability** — promotes artifacts from "fields on a run JSON blob" to first-class product surfaces. Phase 3.3 visualization, Phase 3.4 lessons, Phase 4.1 observer/replay, Phase 6 telemetry import, and Phase 7 calibration all consume this substrate; without it each of them has to invent their own. (LONG-ARC § Protected Seams 3, VISION.md § Visualization Vision.)
- **Regulation-family-aware execution** — strips 2026-only hardcoding from the runtime path so the multi-regulation comparison vision (VISION.md § Multi-Regulation Scope) is not retrofitted against locked 2026 assumptions. (LONG-ARC § Protected Seams 2.)
- **Puzzle / skill-practice seam preservation** (added 2026-05-15) — the `ArtifactEnvelope` shape designed in this phase must accommodate the future "puzzle source" artifact role: selective masking metadata, scoring artifact subtype, difficulty metadata slot. Without this, the chess.com-style puzzle/practice direction added to VISION.md § Skill Practice & Puzzle Mode requires a schema retrofit later. (LONG-ARC § Protected Seams 4 added 2026-05-15, `.planning/initiatives/seeds/puzzle-mode-pedagogy.md`.)
- **Educational coupling through artifacts** — by typing artifacts with fidelity and validation state, this phase makes lesson and visualization surfaces obligate-to-honesty rather than free to invent ad-hoc honesty labeling. (LONG-ARC § Protected Seams 4 original, D3 acceptance.)

**Milestone-only temptation this phase resists:**

The fast path is to "split runService.ts and call it done." That delivers the Phase 3.2 success criteria but leaves async semantics, durable artifact fetch, regulation snapshot identity, and the future puzzle-mode masking story all implicit — re-creating the same fire-and-return architectural debt one layer deeper. The corridor (3.2 → 3.3 → 3.4) is critically chained precisely because these are seam decisions, not just refactoring.

</vision_impact>

<future_awareness>
## Future Awareness

### Protected Seams
- Preserve a transport-neutral execution boundary where the backend consumes compiled requests rather than raw scenario documents or request-handler-only helpers.
- Preserve a first-class artifact identity model with lineage, provenance, fidelity, validation, and regulation references that later visualization, lesson, replay, and import phases can consume without re-keying everything.
- Preserve the raw regulation document -> canonical semantic form -> execution snapshot pipeline so multi-family work later does not have to reverse out 2026-only runtime assumptions.
- Preserve a distinction between run lineage, job identity, artifact identity, and future branch/replay state so later observer and replay work is not forced into the Phase 3.1 run-record shape.
- **Preserve puzzle/practice-mode slots in the artifact envelope** (added 2026-05-15): the `ArtifactEnvelope` shape must accommodate (a) a selective-masking metadata block — which components of an artifact may be hidden from a learner until reveal — (b) artifact subtype tags for `puzzle-source`, `scoring`, `judgment-result`, and `reconstruction-attempt` roles, and (c) a difficulty metadata slot. These are SLOT preservations, not delivery: this phase reserves the field positions and documents them; the puzzle-mode initiative (seed at `.planning/initiatives/seeds/puzzle-mode-pedagogy.md`) delivers the semantics later. The cost of reserving slots now is a few schema fields; the cost of retrofitting against locked artifacts later is much larger.

### Explicit Non-Decisions
- Do not decide the final remote/cloud topology or hosting model.
- Do not decide the long-term scientific-compute language/runtime cutover.
- Do not decide the full cross-family comparability/applicability registry beyond the execution-facing slice needed now.
- Do not decide the full D2 anchor/addressability model; only preserve the artifact identity seams D2 and D3 will need.
- Do not widen this phase into a user-facing shell rewrite or lesson-delivery phase.

### Current Posture
- Local-first and single-user remain the active operating posture.
- The first backend implementation should stay TypeScript and local-worker-backed; bridge architecture is acceptable while rewrite triggers remain unmet.
- Reduced-order, explicit-about-limits execution remains the governing modeling posture; async jobs must not imply calibrated or higher-fidelity physics.
- Existing saved runs and local persistence remain part of the product contract and cannot be discarded casually during artifact migration.

### Future Shape Notes
- Phase 3.3 will need stable artifact identity, partial/final status, and visible provenance/fidelity hooks, but it should not have to care whether the first backend is local-worker, sidecar, or remote.
- Phase 3.4 will need regulation family identity, variant lineage, and semantic applicability slots preserved through artifacts and execution snapshots, even if the broader registry lands later.
- Phase 4.1 replay and observer work will need durable artifact fetch, job progress/checkpoint semantics, and clearer separation between execution events and read-model surfaces.
- Later import/calibration work will need stored raw-plus-compiled regulation lineage and fetchable artifacts, not only inline JSON blobs in run records.
- **Future puzzle / skill-practice initiative** (added 2026-05-15) will need: a `puzzle-source` artifact subtype with selective-masking metadata, separate `scoring` / `judgment-result` / `reconstruction-attempt` artifact subtypes, difficulty metadata on saved runs and scenarios, and (in 3.4) a `puzzle` lesson-body variant. The 3.2 contracts must reserve field positions for the artifact-side slots; full semantics arrive later. See `.planning/initiatives/seeds/puzzle-mode-pedagogy.md` § Architectural implications.

<working_model>
## Working Model & Assumptions

### Current route-return execution is transitional, not the target architecture
- [evidenced:cited] `POST /api/runs` currently dispatches directly to harness-specific service functions, and each path resolves scenario/preset inputs, executes `sim-core` synchronously, builds a `RunRecord`, writes JSON to disk, and returns the full record in one request cycle (`apps/local-api/src/routes/runs.ts`, `apps/local-api/src/services/runService.ts`).
- [evidenced:cited] The browser workspace currently assumes that run creation is synchronous and that the full persisted run record can be reloaded immediately via `GET /api/runs`, with no job handle, progress stream, or durable artifact fetch path (`apps/web/src/features/workspace/api.ts`, `apps/web/src/app/useWorkspace.ts`).
- [governing:cited] Phase 3.2 must treat this fire-and-return path as accepted transitional debt rather than as the stable product surface because D1 closed on compiled requests plus async backend execution (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/01-decision-anchor.md`, `.planning/TECH-DEBT.md` `VA-TD-01`, `VA-TD-03`).

### Request compilation should become its own domain-level seam
- [governing:cited] The durable boundary is `RunCommand -> CompiledSimulationRequest -> SimulationBackend`, with request compilation separated from backend execution and placed in browser-safe domain space rather than buried inside `runService.ts` (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/01-decision-anchor.md`, `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/01-backend-boundary-architecture.md`).
- [evidenced:cited] Current request shaping is spread across `runService.ts`: scenario loading, preset resolution, circuit loading, tire/policy resolution, weather extraction, raw regulation casts, race-plan validation, artifact construction, and persistence are all mixed together (`apps/local-api/src/services/runService.ts`).
- [assumed:reasoned] Planning should treat circuit resolution, tire/policy resolution, scenario snapshot hashing, and regulation execution compilation as compiler responsibilities so the backend interface stays typed and transport-neutral instead of depending on filesystem-only helpers from `apps/local-api`.

### The new stable execution surface is async jobs, not synchronous harness calls
- [governing:cited] The accepted semantic surface is submit/get/cancel/subscribe/listArtifacts/fetchArtifact with typed lifecycle states, sequence-numbered events, artifact-ready notifications, and replay-safe reconnect behavior (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/01-decision-anchor.md`, `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/01-backend-boundary-architecture.md`).
- [evidenced:cited] `packages/sim-core/src/contracts.ts` still exposes a synchronous `SimulationHarness.run(request): SimulationRunSummary` contract with no job identity, progress, cancellation, or artifact fetch semantics.
- [governing:cited] The first backend implementation should be a long-lived local TypeScript worker around the current `sim-core`, which changes the execution contract without forcing immediate language migration (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/01-decision-anchor.md`).
- [assumed:reasoned] The first plan set should bias toward a small async surface that can wrap existing lap, stint, and race kernels before introducing any wider orchestration model, because the main goal is to make D2/D3 consume jobs and artifacts honestly rather than to optimize throughput immediately.

### Artifact identity and storage need to move beyond inline run-record blobs
- [governing:cited] Durable outputs should move to a typed `ArtifactEnvelope` model with immutable `artifactId`, stable `artifactKey`, lineage (`runId`, `runFamilyId`, parent/checkpoint/revision semantics), and provenance including fidelity tier, validation state, regulation references, and semantic applicability slots (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/01-decision-anchor.md`, `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/01-backend-boundary-architecture.md`).
- [evidenced:cited] Current `RunArtifact` shape only includes `artifactId`, `artifactType`, `label`, optional `location`/`mediaType`, and optional inline `data`, and the run services currently persist raw traces and race timelines directly inside the run JSON (`packages/domain/src/runs/schema.ts`, `apps/local-api/src/services/runService.ts`).
- [assumed:reasoned] Planning should preserve the existing logical artifact roles (`qss-speed-profile`, `stint-trace`, `electrical-state-trace`, `weather-evolution-trace`, `race-timeline`) while changing identity, fetch, and provenance semantics underneath; that keeps the 3.3/3.4 corridor from coupling to transient Phase 1-3 storage details.

### Regulation execution must be compiled before runtime, not inferred during execution
- [governing:cited] Backend execution should receive only an `ExecutionRegulationSnapshot`, while runs preserve both raw authored regulation input and compiled execution lineage for reproducibility, including the stable semantic identifiers, override lineage, and canonical semantic reference D5 requires to survive the compile-to-execution handshake (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/01-decision-anchor.md`, `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/04-decision-anchor.md`).
- [evidenced:cited] Current execution still reads raw preset `values` with casts in `runService.ts`, hardcodes 2026 electrical limits in `packages/sim-core/src/stintModel/electricalModel.ts`, and hardcodes no-refueling behavior in the race runner (`apps/local-api/src/services/runService.ts`, `packages/sim-core/src/stintModel/electricalModel.ts`, `packages/sim-core/src/raceModel/raceRunner.ts`).
- [assumed:reasoned] Phase 3.2 should compile only the execution-facing slice now: source preset identity/hash, family identity, optional variant lineage, override lineage, canonical semantic reference, stable semantic identifiers for comparison-relevant concepts, and the concrete capability states needed by current lap/stint/race execution. Broader cross-family applicability and comparison registries belong to the later 3.4/Phase 8 path.

### The orchestration split is about seams, not about hiding `runService.ts` under another wrapper
- [evidenced:cited] `runService.ts` currently mixes scenario I/O, request shaping, execution dispatch, artifact fan-out, lineage passthrough, and persistence for phase1/lap/stint/race paths in one file.
- [assumed:reasoned] Resolving `VA-TD-01` requires a real separation into compiler, backend adapter, and run/artifact persistence concerns; a shallow extraction that leaves raw preset interpretation and execution coupling in the route-service layer will not clear the corridor for 3.3 and 3.4.
- [governing:cited] Existing lap, stint, and race execution behaviors already landed in Phase 3/3.1 are compatibility obligations for this migration, not reasons to bypass the D1 boundary work (`.planning/ROADMAP.md` Phase 3.2, `apps/local-api/src/__tests__/raceRuns.test.ts`).

### Tooling posture pivot (added 2026-05-15)
- [evidenced:cited] `.planning/STATE.md` § "GSD Ecosystem Pivot (2026-05-15)" records the migration from GSDR to mainline classical GSD-1 v1.42.2; only `gsdr:deliberate` / `gsdr:signal` / `gsdr:reflect` / `gsdr:collect-signals` survive on the GSDR side.
- [evidenced:cited] `.planning/config.json` still declares `runtime.platform: codex` and `default_model: gpt-5.4`, and its `runtime.agent_overrides` block references mostly fictional or now-pruned `gsdr-*` agents. The block is documented as orphan in STATE.md but has not been migrated, so downstream agents should treat it as informational legacy, not active runtime intent.
- [assumed:reasoned] Phase 3.2 work must remain runtime-agnostic and agent-agnostic at the planning, code, schema, and artifact level. The execution-boundary contracts (`CompiledSimulationRequest`, `SimulationBackend`, `JobLifecycle`, `ArtifactEnvelope`, `ExecutionRegulationSnapshot`) are independent of which agent or LLM ran a given task; plan-phase agents should not encode model IDs, reasoning levels, or agent overrides into product code or persisted artifact shapes.
- [governing:cited] CLAUDE.md `Quality Gates` and `Phase Planning Expectations` remain authoritative regardless of tooling — typecheck/test/build at root, cross-phase regression checks, benchmark evidence where applicable, and `codex exec review` (not the now-N/A `/gsdr:audit`) for the cross-model audit gate. The `gsdr-verifier` reference in CLAUDE.md was corrected to `gsd-verifier` on 2026-05-15.

<constraints>
## Derived Constraints

1. `packages/sim-core/src/contracts.ts` is still synchronous and summary-only, so Phase 3.2 cannot honestly claim D1 closure without replacing or superseding that contract.
2. `apps/local-api/src/routes/runs.ts` exposes only `GET /runs` and `POST /runs`; there is no job resource, event stream, cancel path, or artifact fetch API yet.
3. `createRunRecord()` and `runRecordSchema` already persist scenario snapshots, preset references/snapshots, lineage fields, and artifacts; Phase 3.2 should reuse that reproducibility discipline even if `run-record/v2` is needed.
4. `packages/domain/src/scenario/raceSchema.ts` and the race-run tests show that Phase 3.1 already added `racePlan`, `parentRunId`, `branchPoint`, `experimentTag`, and race artifacts; planning must preserve those authored inputs and persisted semantics while migrating execution.
5. Current run records still treat artifacts as part of the run JSON blob. Durable fetch semantics imply either a manifest/reference layer, a hybrid legacy/new shape, or both.
6. The preset schema remains `values: Record<string, unknown>`, which is flexible for authored raw documents but unacceptable as the backend-facing execution contract.
7. 2026-specific execution assumptions currently live in both the route-service layer and `sim-core`; Phase 3.2 must remove them from the runtime path that later phases consume, not merely add more typed casts around them.
8. The browser workspace currently offers lap and stint run creation only; Phase 3.2 may need adapter surfaces for job-backed execution, but it should not widen into the full 3.3 shell/substrate rewrite.
9. Existing tests in `apps/local-api/src/__tests__/raceRuns.test.ts`, `packages/sim-core/src/__tests__/raceModel.test.ts`, and related run-path tests are part of the safety net. Planning should assume regression coverage matters because 3.2 is changing architecture, not inventing one isolated new feature.
10. Local file persistence is part of the current product contract. Even if jobs and artifacts get new stores/manifests, the first implementation still needs a coherent local persistence story.

</constraints>

<guardrails>
## Epistemic Guardrails

1. Do not treat the existence of `race-simulation` and raw race artifacts as proof that the backend boundary already exists. Those are current payloads behind the old synchronous path.
2. Async jobs must not be framed as a performance or cloud feature. In this phase they are an honesty and architecture requirement so later phases stop pretending that synchronous route returns are the stable compute substrate.
3. `ExecutionRegulationSnapshot` is an execution-facing contract, not the full D5 semantic ontology. Preserve the D5 axes that must survive compilation, including stable semantic identifiers, override lineage, and a canonical semantic reference, but do not widen Phase 3.2 into the full registry/applicability/comparison phase.
4. Artifact durability means reconnect-safe fetch and stable identity, not merely adding more fields to inline JSON blobs.
5. Partial/progress artifacts do not imply calibrated or validated results. Fidelity tier, validation state, and provenance remain visible architectural obligations.
6. Migration plans must account for existing saved runs and tests explicitly. Silent breakage or undocumented one-way conversion would violate the repo's reproducibility posture.
7. Keep 3.3 and 3.4 responsibilities out of this phase except where their future consumption clearly constrains artifact identity, provenance, or regulation lineage.

</guardrails>

<questions>
## Open Questions

### 1. What is the minimum useful `CompiledSimulationRequest` family for Phase 3.2?
- **Why it matters:** The compiler seam is the backbone of the new boundary. Planning needs to decide whether lap/stint/race submit one discriminated request family, or whether execution is split more narrowly by workload type.
- **Downstream decision:** Domain execution types, compiler module layout, and how much pre-resolution lives outside the backend.
- **Reversibility:** Medium — request families shape most of the boundary code.
- **Research should:** Compare one top-level discriminated request union versus more modular compiler outputs, using the current lap/stint/race paths and future Phase 4.1/6 consumers as the stress cases.

### 2. What is the first job-topology model: one job per run, parent/child jobs, or a lighter `runFamily` grouping?
- **Why it matters:** D1 explicitly left the batch-shape question open. Progress, cancellation, lineage, and future multi-strategy runs all depend on how much structure the first job model carries.
- **Downstream decision:** Job IDs, cancellation rules, event stream payloads, and whether `runFamilyId` belongs in the first implementation.
- **Reversibility:** Medium.
- **Research should:** Start from the accepted D1 semantics and evaluate the simplest model that still keeps future multi-run comparison and branch lineage honest.

### 3. What is the first credible migration shape from inline `runRecord.artifacts[].data` to durable artifact fetch semantics?
- **Why it matters:** D1 accepts a likely `run-record/v2` or hybrid migration, but the repo already has saved runs and tests using the current inline shape.
- **Downstream decision:** Whether planning introduces manifest-only artifacts, hybrid legacy/new artifacts, read-through compatibility, or a staged migration.
- **Reversibility:** Low to medium — once persisted, migration shape becomes sticky.
- **Research should:** Map the current run-record reads in API and web code, then evaluate the smallest migration that unlocks fetchable artifacts without losing local readability.

### 4. What is the minimum `ExecutionRegulationSnapshot` field set that clears the 3.2 corridor without stealing 3.4?
- **Why it matters:** The execution compiler needs enough identity and capability structure to remove raw-preset and 2026-only runtime assumptions now, but broader comparability/applicability work belongs later.
- **Downstream decision:** Snapshot schema, runtime compile step, run provenance fields, and which current `sim-core` constants/config paths must be parameterized immediately.
- **Reversibility:** Medium.
- **Research should:** Trace every current regulation-dependent branch in lap/stint/race execution and derive the smallest stable capability slice plus the D5 identity, semantic-reference, and override-lineage fields that must survive compilation.

### 5. What should the first local edge transport look like while the browser remains on the old workspace shell?
- **Why it matters:** D1 closed semantically on REST + SSE, but the current client only knows synchronous create-and-refresh behavior. Phase 3.2 likely needs an adapter path that does not pull the whole D2 shell rewrite forward.
- **Downstream decision:** Route/resource layout, initial browser adapter strategy, and replay-window semantics for reconnect.
- **Reversibility:** High.
- **Research should:** Define the thinnest job/event/artifact API that can coexist with the current local-first workspace while still making the D1 contract real.

### 6. How much artifact addressability belongs in Phase 3.2 versus Phase 3.3?
- **Why it matters:** D1 leaves `anchorSchema` depth open, while D2/D3 will need stable artifact-relative addressing later.
- **Downstream decision:** Whether 3.2 only reserves the seam (`artifactKey`, lineage, revision, optional addressability slot) or also formalizes a first manifest of stable anchor entities now.
- **Reversibility:** Medium.
- **Research should:** Separate what D2 needs on day one from what can remain payload-local until renderer and lesson bindings actually exist.

</questions>

## Specific Ideas

- Keep the current `/api/runs` listing as a read-model surface while introducing a job-oriented submit/status/artifact path beside it, rather than forcing all consumers onto a new model in one cut.
- Treat the first local worker as an adapter around the already-landed lap/stint/race kernels, not as a rewrite of those kernels.
- Compile regulation execution state into explicit capability modules such as electrical, aero, refueling/session constraints, and tire-allocation-related execution inputs, while retaining raw authored preset snapshots separately.
- Let artifact manifests carry stable logical roles and revisions even if the first implementation still stores payloads locally on disk.

### Puzzle-mode seam preservation (added 2026-05-15)

When designing the `ArtifactEnvelope` and execution-domain types in plan 03.2-01 and the persistence/envelope layer in plan 03.2-03, reserve schema field positions for puzzle/practice-mode metadata:

- A `masking` block on `ArtifactEnvelope` describing which artifact components may be selectively hidden from a learner (the field can be `null` or absent in non-puzzle artifacts; what matters is that adding it later is non-breaking).
- Artifact subtype tags that accommodate future `puzzle-source`, `scoring`, `judgment-result`, `reconstruction-attempt` roles alongside the current `qss-speed-profile`, `stint-trace`, `electrical-state-trace`, `weather-evolution-trace`, `race-timeline` roles.

These are slot reservations only. The puzzle-mode initiative seeded at `.planning/initiatives/seeds/puzzle-mode-pedagogy.md` delivers semantics later. The cost of reserving fields now is small; the cost of retrofitting locked artifacts is large. See `<future_awareness>` § Protected Seams (puzzle/practice-mode slot) and `<vision_impact>`.
