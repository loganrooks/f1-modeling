# 05 Decision Anchor

## D4 outcome

D4 closes provisionally but decisively on roadmap shape: the current roadmap is no longer structurally honest after D1/D2/D3/D5, and Phase 4 planning should stay paused until explicit pre-Phase-4 foundation insertions exist for backend execution and artifacts, visualization substrate and shell, and educational plus minimal semantic groundwork. The key roadmap consequence is that accepted architecture must now become sequencing rather than commentary: v1 should be reshaped around an honest single-family engineering-and-education loop, v2 should deepen compute and semantic breadth, v3 is the clearest point where the platform comes into its own as a calibrated comparative engineering lab, and v4+ is where operational/product expansion such as live race-weekend use or collaboration can honestly live.

## Most important Phase 4 planning implication

Phase 4 must stop pretending to be the place where substrate gets invented. Once planning resumes, Phase 4 should be a narrowed strategy workbench and session-workflow phase that consumes already-landed job/artifact contracts, recipe-driven shell and interaction state, anchor semantics, lesson schema/preview/validation, and minimal regulation canonicalization. If Stage 3 does not insert that foundation work before Phase 4, Phase 4 planning will reintroduce the exact synchronous, shell-bound, content-light assumptions Wave 2 just rejected.

## Most important Stage 3A roadmap implication

Stage 3A should not merely rename current phases. It needs to propose explicit insertions between Phase 3.1 and Phase 4, narrow and possibly rename Phase 4, make the likely Phase 5 control split visible, keep Phase 6 and Phase 7 as the reality-contact corridor while acknowledging earlier honesty obligations, and narrow Phase 8 to the first semantically grounded regulation/design exploration wave rather than full multi-era breadth. It also needs to add high-level v2 / v3 / v4+ milestone themes so the scientific-compute migration story, historical regulation expansion, and later operational product expansion stop living only in deliberation prose.

## Most important Stage 3B guardrail / tech-debt implication

Stage 3B must create the guardrails and tech-debt registry that D4 now depends on: benchmark enforcement from the R1.5 envelopes, visible fidelity/validation/comparability labeling, a freeze on new legacy-SVG feature growth, explicit rewrite triggers for `runService.ts`, the synchronous harness contract, the current three-zone shell, and raw-preset-driven regulation execution, plus the phase-gate question of whether planned work serves the long-horizon vision or only the immediate milestone. Without that registry, the roadmap diff will still understate the rewrite pressure D4 is explicitly surfacing.

## Most important unresolved question for future milestone audits

The largest unresolved question D4 leaves is when the bridge architecture for computation stops being enough. D4 does not close the final long-term compute language/runtime, because that threshold is empirical rather than ideological: future milestone audits need to ask whether Phase 4.1 observer workloads, Phase 5 optimization demands, or Phase 7 calibration loops have crossed the accepted latency, batch, or authoring-workflow thresholds strongly enough that v2 must become an explicit scientific-compute migration milestone rather than only a deferred possibility.

## Disposition on D4.A-D

D4.A is closed at scope level: new pre-Phase-4 insertions are required and should remain distinct rather than folded into one vague foundation wave, even though exact numbering is left to Stage 3A. D4.B is provisional: the remaining v1 path is clearly mis-cut in Phases 4, 5, and 8, but exact boundary text still belongs to the roadmap diff stage. D4.C is provisional by design: the roadmap should project to v4+ thematically, with v3 as the clearest "comes into its own" threshold, but not with implementation-plan granularity. D4.D is closed at handoff level: major deferrals, rewrite triggers, and Stage 3 outputs are explicit enough that the next stage can produce roadmap diffs, guardrails, tech-debt artifacts, and summary materials without reopening D1-D5.
