## Weaknesses Or Gaps

- R6 is the clearest empirical gap. It is appropriately honest, but the decisive numbers are still unmeasured, especially single-race wall time, batch makespan, and memory ceilings (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/research/06-performance-envelopes.md:149-156`, `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/research/06-performance-envelopes.md:307-316`, `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/research/06-performance-envelopes.md:379-386`).
- R3 is careful about precedent opacity, but that also makes part of its warrant weaker. It explicitly says Brilliant and Khan Academy expose product behavior more than inspectable internal schema or authoring models, so the strongest architectural evidence in that lane comes from Observable, MyST, and Distill instead (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/research/03-educational-content-architectures.md:275-293`, `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/research/03-educational-content-architectures.md:406-416`).
- R2 reached the right architectural terrain but not interface completeness. Later synthesis still had to carry `AccessibleChartContract` and `AnchorRegistry` as explicit debt. That is more of a handoff-completeness gap than a research-method failure, but it is still the clearest place where “good terrain map” did not by itself become “implementation-safe contract surface” (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/research/02-visualization-at-scale.md:390-396`; `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/synthesis/tech-debt-registry.md:22-25`).

## What It Likely Missed

- The current pipeline does not explicitly rank the individual research artifacts by epistemic strength. That matters because the corpus is not flat: R1/R4/R5 are stronger on direct warrant than R6, and parts of R3 are more precedent-limited than the compute/regulation lanes.
- The pipeline mostly treats “research quality” and “implementation-handoff completeness” together. Inference: the D2 follow-on debt suggests a missing intermediate question, namely which research concepts still need explicit interface closure before code can safely start (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/research/02-visualization-at-scale.md:390-396`; `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/synthesis/tech-debt-registry.md:22-25`).
- The transcript extracts report no major blind spots in extraction coverage, but that is coverage assurance, not precedent-quality assurance. It does not answer which research claims rest on inspectable internals versus public-facing product behavior (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/debrief/extracts/claude-chat-extract.md:33-35`; `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/debrief/extracts/codex-chat-extract.md:33-35`).

## Deterministic Implications

- Treat the Wave 1 / 1.5 research corpus as trustworthy terrain maps for planning and synthesis. That is already the basis Stage 3A used to insert Phases `3.2`, `3.3`, and `3.4` (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/synthesis/roadmap-evolution.md:51-75`).
- Do not treat R6 numbers as validated truth. The benchmark/reporting path remains a hard carry-forward obligation, not optional polish (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/research/06-performance-envelopes.md:355-386`; `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/synthesis/tech-debt-registry.md:21-22`).
- Do not let D2/D3 substrate implementation proceed while `AccessibleChartContract` and `AnchorRegistry` remain implicit. The research was good enough to identify the need, but not to close those interfaces (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/02-decision-anchor.md:11-29`; `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/03-decision-anchor.md:7-10`, `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/deliberations/03-decision-anchor.md:19-25`; `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/synthesis/tech-debt-registry.md:23-24`).
- Preserve the research prompt shape that worked: code-first grounding, forced assumptions, explicit reframing, gray-area tagging, path/dependency sections, and confidence ledgers. Those behaviors created the reusable downstream leverage (`.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/RESEARCH-PRINCIPLES.md:91-147`, `.planning/initiatives/vision-alignment-2026-04/RESEARCH-PRINCIPLES.md:224-315`).

## Confidence And Limits

- High confidence: the corpus adhered to the initiative’s research principles in practice and materially shaped later deliberation, roadmap, and governance artifacts.
- Medium confidence: the relative ranking between R2, R3, and R5 is partly judgmental because those files solve different questions with different evidence surfaces.
- This lane evaluated the research record as documented. It did not independently re-audit every external source behind each research file; the claim here is that the initiative documented uncertainty and evidence quality honestly enough to be reusable.
