# Neglect And Counterevidence

**Status:** Completed on 2026-04-16.  
**Basis:** Hardened transcript substrate, residual miss audit, validation A/C/D, and current debrief outputs.

## Bottom Line

The strongest remaining risk is not hidden contradictory evidence; it is confidence inflation. The retained transcript pack is auditable and useful, but it is still windowed, non-exhaustive, and concentrated in a few interaction families. On current evidence, the initiative's main architecture and live-file consequences still stand, but several clean-close claims need narrower wording, two accepted directions were carried forward rather than applied, and some uncertainty should remain open rather than being summarized away.

## Verified

- The hardened transcript substrate is usable for bounded synthesis only if it stays explicitly non-exhaustive. `NEGLECT-REVIEW.md` still shows duplicate-fingerprint clusters in Claude and cross-model pools, `artifacts/transcript-nlp/residual-miss-audit.md` says the remaining work is interpretive rather than corpus-complete, and `specs/validation/report-validation-A-coverage-and-neglect.md` confirms the extracts are selected samples rather than a raw-session reread.
- No reviewed counterevidence overturns the initiative's main direction or the core live applications. `specs/validation/report-validation-D-live-file-application-verification.md` directly verifies the roadmap reshape, the `.planning/TECH-DEBT.md` registry, the `CLAUDE.md` guardrail changes, and the `.planning/STATE.md` routing shift.
- The main negative process findings are real, not summary invention. `FINDINGS.md` and `INTERACTION-REVIEW.md` still support implicit-authorization overrun, recommendation drift before re-analysis, detached-execution trust damage, and the D4 advisory-vs-formal audit failure.

## Soften

- Interaction claims should stay at "in the reviewed evidence" scope. `specs/validation/report-validation-A-coverage-and-neglect.md` specifically rejects stronger corpus-wide wording such as "came primarily from" and "only became reliable after it was written down" unless those claims are narrowed to the retained sample.
- Any claim that D2/D3 fully closed the visualization and education interface surface should be softened. `specs/validation/report-validation-C-skipped-branches-and-non-events.md` shows the optional `2B-iii` D2 revision never ran, and later artifacts had to carry `AccessibleChartContract` and `AnchorRegistry` closure forward instead.
- Any claim that the initiative changed broader planning templates or operating doctrine at closeout should be softened. `specs/validation/report-validation-D-live-file-application-verification.md` proves application to `.planning/ROADMAP.md`, `.planning/TECH-DEBT.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, and `.planning/STATE.md`; it does not prove template-level propagation beyond those files.
- Any causal claim that the initiative succeeded mainly because the method worked should be softened. `runs/2026-04-16-llm-sweep/reports/H-neglect-and-counterevidence.md` supports a narrower claim: the method mattered, but success also depended on explicit recoveries after real failures.

That is a design limit of the windowed extract pack, not a disproved claim (`INTERACTION-REVIEW.md`; `runs/2026-04-16-llm-sweep/reports/H-neglect-and-counterevidence.md`).

- The heavy transcript intermediates are not retained, so deeper replay of candidate-to-window selection would require rerunning the pipeline, not just rereading the canon (`specs/validation/report-validation-A-coverage-and-neglect.md`).
- Artifact/provenance may still be underemphasized relative to process failures. The structure review elevated it as cross-cutting, but the debrief synthesis still foregrounds process more than typed artifact lineage and contract consequences (`runs/2026-04-16-llm-sweep/reports/H-neglect-and-counterevidence.md`; `DEBRIEF-SUMMARY.md`).

## What The Hardened Transcript Substrate Likely Still Misses

- Distribution, not just existence, of steering burden: the extracts preserve correction moments but do not tell us how dominant they were across the full session universe (`specs/validation/report-validation-A-coverage-and-neglect.md`; `INTERACTION-REVIEW.md`).
- Less representative cross-model interaction families: the residual miss audit and `NEGLECT-REVIEW.md` both warn that the cross-model lane is concentrated toward one emitted family and should not be read as a proportional sample (`artifacts/transcript-nlp/residual-miss-audit.md`; `NEGLECT-REVIEW.md`).
- Wrapper and launch-semantics behavior outside the sampled cross-model lane: delegation evidence exists, but chat-lane suppression means some orchestration behavior is still underrepresented (`specs/validation/report-validation-A-coverage-and-neglect.md`).
- Negative-evidence sampling across omitted sessions: the current substrate is strong on selected moments and explicit miss flags, not on proving that no materially contrary moments exist elsewhere (`NEGLECT-REVIEW.md`; `artifacts/transcript-nlp/residual-miss-audit.md`).

## What The Initiative Did Not Apply Despite Accepted Direction

- It did not use the optional `2B-iii` revision branch to close D2/D3 interface completeness inside the initiative. The accepted substrate direction stood, but `AccessibleChartContract` and `AnchorRegistry` were left as explicit later obligations instead of being closed in-wave (`specs/validation/report-validation-C-skipped-branches-and-non-events.md`; `MODEL-ROLE-AND-DIVISION-OF-LABOR.md`).
- It did not prove template-level propagation of the accepted guardrails at closeout. The verified application set is narrower: roadmap, tech-debt registry, `CLAUDE.md`, and state routing (`specs/validation/report-validation-D-live-file-application-verification.md`).
- It did not earn exhaustive transcript-confidence language. The hardened pipeline improved the evidence base, but the residual miss audit and validation A both require bounded-confidence wording to remain in the top-level debrief (`artifacts/transcript-nlp/residual-miss-audit.md`; `specs/validation/report-validation-A-coverage-and-neglect.md`).

## Closeout Disposition

Spec H does not overturn the main debrief. It narrows it. The canonical closeout posture should be: the initiative materially improved live planning state and direction; the transcript evidence is useful but non-exhaustive; process failures were real and often recoverable; interface-completeness and broader propagation claims need explicit carry-forward or proof rather than summary inflation.
