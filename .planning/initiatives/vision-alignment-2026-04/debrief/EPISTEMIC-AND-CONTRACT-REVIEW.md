# Epistemic And Contract Review

**Status:** Scaffold only. Populate during the debrief run.
**Source spec:** `specs/spec-debrief-E-epistemic-quality-and-contract-adherence.md`

## Purpose

Evaluate whether the initiative's research and deliberations were trustworthy on their own terms, not just whether they happened to produce useful downstream outcomes.

This review should answer:

- did the research meet the epistemic and methodological requirements in `../RESEARCH-PRINCIPLES.md`?
- did the deliberations remain reliable, traceable, and contract-shaped rather than drifting into label-level closure?
- did the work honor the assignments and cross-cutting constraints in `../BOUNDARY-CONTRACT-MEMO.md`?
- did execution meet the original goals of the orchestrator handoffs?
- where did the initiative deviate, and were those deviations justified?
