# Interaction Review

**Status:** Scaffold only. Populate after transcript extraction.
**Source spec:** `specs/spec-debrief-F-interaction-review.md`

## Purpose

Evaluate the initiative as an interaction process, not just a document process.

This review should cover:

- moments of frustration, confusion, or repeated clarification
- moments where user interaction improved the work materially
- authorization and delegation ambiguity
- how Codex and Claude differed in role, contribution, and quality
- what subagents were used, and whether their use helped or hindered

## Inputs

Primary inputs should be the extracted evidence packs in `extracts/`, not raw transcript stores.
