# Findings

**Status:** Scaffold only. Populate during the debrief run.
**Source specs:** `specs/spec-debrief-B-process-and-auditability-review.md`, `specs/spec-debrief-C-substantive-value-and-patterns-to-keep.md`

## Purpose

Capture the main debrief findings in a single evidence-backed ledger before they are translated into carry-forward actions.

## Rules

- Every finding needs a claim, evidence, consequence, and proposed disposition.
- Separate substantive outcome findings from process and auditability findings.
- Use "near-failure" when the problem was real but did not become a full failure because it was caught or contained in time.
